Our Story

Based in Sialkot, Pakistan, Imzagi Intl is a dedicated manufacturer and exporter of premium apparel and sportswear. With expertise in producing everything from professional WWE-style championship belts to custom uniforms, hoodies, jackets, and tracksuits, we serve an international clientele with quality craftsmanship.

Our commitment to custom branding and personalized solutions has made us a trusted partner for businesses worldwide seeking reliable manufacturing and export services from Pakistan.
